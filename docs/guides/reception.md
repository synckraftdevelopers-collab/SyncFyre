# Reception guide

## Start of shift

1. Sign in and confirm you are on the correct branch.
2. Review the dashboard for today’s appointments, pending payments, and expiring memberships.
3. Keep the member search open for quick check-in and profile access.

## Register a member

1. Open **Members** and select **Add member**.
2. Enter contact information, choose the branch, and record only relevant health details.
3. Select an active package, start date, and payment amount.
4. Confirm the invoice and receipt details before saving.
5. Use the member profile to upload a photo or add notes when required.

## Payments and renewals

1. Open **Payments** to find pending invoices.
2. Record the correct payment method and transaction reference.
3. Confirm the balance shown after saving.
4. For renewals, use the member profile and select the next membership package.
5. Never change a completed payment to conceal an error; ask a manager to correct it.

## Appointments and attendance

- Confirm pending appointments, then mark them completed or cancelled after the visit.
- Use the attendance screen for manual corrections only when the biometric/device record is unavailable.
- Report unmatched biometric events or device issues to a manager.

## End of shift

- Check for unrecorded payments and pending appointments.
- Confirm cash/UPI/card totals against the recorded payment list.
- Sign out from shared devices.
