# Manager guide

## Daily operating review

1. Sign in and choose the correct branch context.
2. Review dashboard totals for members, attendance, renewals, revenue, and outstanding balances.
3. Follow up on expiring memberships, overdue invoices, and equipment due for maintenance.

## Member and membership controls

- Review member registrations and branch assignments.
- Approve or correct membership changes, renewals, and payment exceptions.
- Deactivate members only when policy requires it; deletion/deactivation affects future operational visibility.
- Keep branch information accurate so staff and member records remain correctly scoped.

## Finance and reports

1. Review payments and invoices for the selected branch.
2. Compare outstanding balances with receipts and collection records.
3. Use the reports screens for member, attendance, payment, and revenue filters.
4. Export only the minimum data required and store exports securely.

## Staff oversight

- Review appointments and attendance corrections made by reception.
- Resolve device, biometric, and member-mapping escalations.
- Ensure reception users work only in their assigned branch.
- Escalate access, tenant, role, or RLS issues to an administrator.

## Security

- Do not share accounts or service credentials.
- Sign out of shared terminals.
- Treat member health/contact data and exported reports as confidential.
- Report suspicious sign-in attempts or unexpected branch data immediately.
